# The-DRAGON-a-very-fast-and-versatile-car-
 It has a trapezium-looking shape from the side, with 2 scissor doors, a completely vertical sides, and a spoiler on the descending rear panel that has the top bit at roof height, and there are 3 small fins on top in a tadpole (reverse delta) arrangement, and the back is slightly narrower than the front; so the areodynamics should be amazing.

It has a 2L V4 engine mid mounted, with a centrifugal supercharger. It revs to 10,000RPM and produces 200kW aimed. It drives the rear. There is a 160kW electric motor mounted front, driving the front. The V4 is mated to a 9-speed hybrid manual (you can paddle shift or stick shift). 

It has 3 seats (2 front, 1 rear), which are made of a titanium frame, merino wool filling, and hemp fibre cover. The steering wheel is made of recycled bamboo, and wrapped in alcantara. The trim is hemp fibre and pink-anodized recycled magnesium.

In theory, it can be used for: 
1. An extremely fun weekend sports car
2. A GT-style circuit and endurance racer
3. A rally car
